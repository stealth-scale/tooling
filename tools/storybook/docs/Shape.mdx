# Shape

Two decisions make a system feel soft or precise: how much its corners are rounded, and how
things lift off the page. Both come from one value of the theme's each, so they cannot drift
apart.

## Radius

A theme sets one `--radius`. Every step below is a multiple of it, which is why a theme cannot
round its cards and leave its buttons square. Switch the theme in the toolbar to see the whole
scale move.

<Radii />

A component picks a step by meaning, not by measurement: `rounded-md` on a control,
`rounded-lg` on a container, `rounded-full` on an avatar. A pixel value survives a theme change
unchanged and stands out.

## Elevation

Every shadow mixes from the theme's shadow ink, so a shadow that lifts a card off white does
not vanish against near-black. In dark mode every box shadow also carries a hairline of light
along its top edge, because there a shadow is ink on ink and what reads as an edge is light
catching the top. A glow is thrown in the theme's glow colour rather than its ink: no offset
and a wide blur, so it reads as light coming off a surface.

<Shadows />

Use `shadow-sm` for something resting on the page, `shadow-md` for something temporarily above
it, `shadow-lg` for something that has taken over, such as a dialog or a popover with real
content.
