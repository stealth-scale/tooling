Groups the contrast table by the kind of pair a reader is looking for, rather than printing all 34 text guarantees under one heading. The four groups are a `-foreground` on the token it pairs with, an `-ink` on the page, a `-soft-foreground` on its `-soft` fill, and a syntax role on `muted`. Each filter reads the suffix, so a token added to the contract needs no edit to the page.

Draws `motion-state` and `motion-press` on the Motion page beside a third control with no class on it, since a table cannot show a transition.
